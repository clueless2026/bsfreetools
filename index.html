The "No BS" Philosophy

BSFreeTools was built to solve a simple problem: the modern web is cluttered with predatory subscriptions, aggressive tracking scripts, and tools that force you to upload your private files to unknown servers. Our architecture uses modern Progressive Web App (PWA) standards to execute all logic—whether it's splitting a PDF or calculating a financial ledger—directly inside your browser's local cache. We use standard ad placements to keep the lights on, but we will never compromise your data sovereignty.
