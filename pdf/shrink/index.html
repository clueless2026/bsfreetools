How Our Browser-Based PDF Compressor Works

Most free online PDF shrinkers force you to upload your sensitive tax documents, contracts, or IDs to a remote server. This is a massive privacy risk. Our Secure PDF Shrink tool works entirely within your web browser.

We use advanced client-side scripts to rasterize your document and compress the internal images without ever transmitting your data across the internet. Note: This process "flattens" your PDF, making text unsearchable, but it guarantees a drastically smaller file size perfect for strict email attachment limits.
